Frequently asked question

Everything you need to know about our restoration model and operations.

Green Legacy is a climate resilience and agricultural development initiative operating across Northern Nigeria and cross-border corridors like Maradi. We restore degraded land, build resilient crop transport logistics, and provide smallholder farmers with direct access to finance.

We work directly with rural farming cooperatives to plant shelterbelts of Moringa and Guava trees, establish grain aggregation depots along transport routes, and partner with financial institutions to offer credit access.

Organizations, developmental agencies, and private investors can partner with us by funding specific reforestation corridors, providing agricultural inputs, or sponsoring smallholder credit access. Reach out via our contact form to get started.

Green Legacy operates as an integrated social enterprise and field development initiative. We combine philanthropic environmental restoration with sustainable market mechanisms so smallholder farmers generate long-term profit.
